daTextBox=<FONT FACE="courier" SIZE="11" COLOR="#000000">After returning from the East coast I went into the garage and tried to capture what I had seen. I had built furniture as a hobby so I was familiar with the basic aspects of woodworking but never had I tried to create anything as unpolished and full of energy as the art I had seen. I quickly went to work building a four-foot tall man out of some tree limbs. Later that day I showed the finished product to my dinner guests. They couldn't believe that I had built it. 
I quickly went back to work and created three more carvings and presented them to the owner of Down East Gallery and General Store, a gallery specializing in products and works of art created in Atlantic Canada. I wanted to see if I had any talent for this new undertaking and he was just the man to ask. If he said no I would stick to my cabinet making. Lucky for me, he immediately took to the work and wanted some pieces for his own personal collection. I was both honored and excited. Not only did I find folk art enjoyable to create, but also people liked it. So I continued to carve and create whatever popped into my head, selling the odd piece from time to time.
